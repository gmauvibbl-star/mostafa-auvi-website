❦Biography

Mostafa Auvi was born in the village of Boro Bighai, in Patuakhali Sadar Upazila, Bangladesh. By profession he is a banker. He studied Bengali language and literature, and later also studied law. Since 2016 he has been writing regularly. His interests lie in the rural life of Bengal, the history of settlements, human memory, loneliness, love, magical realism, mythic traditions, religious motifs, and the meeting of imagination with reality.

In his writing, history is not a textbook chronicle; rather, the experience of history emerges through human lives, relationships, folk memory and the passage of time. Besides stories and novels, he writes essays, literary criticism, and reflections on the fabric of society.

His first story, “Bajpakhir Punorjonmo” (The Rebirth of the Falcon), was published in 2017 in the Bengali dailies Daily Sonar Desh and Daily Sangbad. That publication marked the beginning of his literary life. Since then he has continued to write stories, essays and literary criticism on a regular basis.

His published books include Bajpakhir Punorjonmo (2017), CS Khotiyan O Ekti Mamlar Itibrityo (2019), and Beraler Quarantine (2022).

❦Recognition & Publications

  • First publication (2017): The story “Bajpakhir Punorjonmo” appeared in the dailies Daily Sonar Desh and Daily Sangbad — the beginning of his literary life.
  • First book (2017): Bajpakhir Punorjonmo — a collection of stories.
