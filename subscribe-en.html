Email updates of new writing

Subscribe to receive news by email when new writing is published. A confirmation email will be sent to verify your subscription. You can unsubscribe at any time.

What you will receive

Subscribe to receive email updates when Mostafa Auvi publishes new writing or announces a book. The newsletter carries news about his fiction, essays, literary criticism, travel writing, translated literature, and other published work.

The website includes short stories, novels, essays on literature and culture, book reviews, writing on law and the courts, and articles on banking and economics. When a new piece or a relevant book announcement is published, subscribers may receive an email about it.

About the email updates

This is not a daily or weekly mailing list. Updates are sent when there is new writing or a relevant announcement. To complete your subscription, you must confirm your email address through the link sent to your inbox. If the message does not arrive, check your Spam or Promotions folder.

An update email may include the title of a published piece, a short description, and a link to read it on the website. It may also carry information about a new book, a new edition, or a literary announcement.

Your email address is used only for these updates. Every update email includes an unsubscribe link, which you can use at any time to stop receiving messages.
