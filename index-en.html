THE AUTHOR

About the Author

Mostafa Auvi is a Bangladeshi fiction writer and literary critic. The riverine landscapes, people, history and memory of southern Bengal inform his writing. Alongside fiction, he writes essays, literary criticism, travel writing, and reflections on law and society.

Chandradwip

A century-long story of a riverine settlement

A novel set in the tidal lowlands of southern Bengal, tracing people, rivers, settlement and history from 1850 to 14 August 1947.
