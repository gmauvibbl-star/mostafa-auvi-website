The Author

Mostafa Auvi is an international fiction writer whose work explores the intersections of culture, memory, and displacement. Born in Dhaka and raised across three continents, his narratives are woven from the threads of his global experiences.

"I do not write to tell the reader what to think. I write to offer a mirror in which they might find the parts of the world they had forgotten to look at."

His works have been translated into over fifteen languages and have received numerous international literary awards. When he is not writing, Auvi spends his time lecturing on creative writing and advocating for freedom of expression through literature.
