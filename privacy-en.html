Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This is Mostafa Auvi’s personal literary website. It shows no ads and runs no analytics or tracking scripts. Here is, in plain words, what it collects.

1. Email subscription

If you subscribe, only your email address is stored. A confirmation email is sent first, and the subscription starts only after you click its link. The address is used solely to send news of new writing, and every email carries an unsubscribe link.

2. Comments

When you comment, the name you enter and your comment text are shown publicly. To prevent spam, an encrypted (hashed) value derived from your connection is kept for a limited time. The raw IP address is not stored.

3. Read counts

To count how often an article is read, only the article and the time are recorded. No name, email or other personal identifier is attached.

4. Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Supabase runs the database and server functions that hold subscriptions and comments.
  • Resend delivers the emails.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces, so your browser’s IP address may reach Google.

Your data is not sold or passed on beyond these providers.

5. Browser storage

To load faster, your browser keeps a cache of some site files. You can clear it in your browser settings.

6. Deletion and contact

To have your email or a comment removed, write to gmauvibbl@gmail.com. You can also unsubscribe with the link at the bottom of any email.

If this policy changes, the new date will appear on this page.
